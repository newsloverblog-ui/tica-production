The working-tree fixes have been verified locally against a disposable MySQL database initialized from the repository’s CI baseline. The original scenario results below record the audit of the candidate commit. The fixes require an isolated staging deployment and the sandbox drills before that verdict can change.

The frontend build and six Chromium smoke checks passed across desktop and mobile organizer, gate and admin views. All three migrations applied on disposable MySQL. The Supabase update script passed repository coverage checks and awaits execution against a staging PostgreSQL copy.

## Launch blockers in priority order

1. **Confirm isolated staging and candidate scope.** Have the release owner identify the candidate and staging URL, deployment ID, SHA, synthetic database, queue/cache isolation, sandbox provider accounts, enabled channels and clients, alert sink, and QA access via the approved secret channel. Compare SHAs, confirm both queue workers and scheduler heartbeats, then run the full matrix on that build. If another commit is staged, record its results separately and rerun against the candidate.
2. **Clear the backend dependency gate (REL-02).** Update `web-token/jwt-library` to a compatible patched release through its dependency constraint, then rerun the locked Composer audit and Platform verification. Preserve clean npm and secret checks, and collect separate transport, session and redaction evidence on staging. [Upstream releases](https://github.com/web-token/jwt-framework/releases) · [Composer lock](../composer.lock#L9720)
3. **Hold payout enablement pending approval, recovery and pause fixes (OUT-04/05/08/10/11).** Require distinct, time-limited approval bound to amount, currency and verified beneficiary. Recheck at dispatch, reconcile stale submissions by their original reference, and decouple the submission pause from recovery. Align failure/reversal ledger behavior with signed Finance policy. Retest approvals and revocation, beneficiary changes, paused queued vs submitted transfers, a sandbox timeout and interruption, event ordering, and provider-to-ledger completeness. [Transfer service](../app/Services/PayoutTransferService.php) · [Payout runbook](../docs/payout-release-runbook.md)
4. **Close the Paystack chargeback and reconciliation gap (PAY-11/12).** Implement an approved, auditable dispute lifecycle and repeat-safe ticket/ledger handling for chargebacks. Verify sandbox dispute outcomes before and after payout, including reconciliation to a completed provider scan and finance ownership of all exceptions. Run the full checkout and refund matrix for every enabled gateway. [Paystack webhook](../app/Http/Controllers/FrontEnd/PaymentGateway/PaystackController.php#L246) · [Launch checklist](../docs/production-launch-evidence-checklist.md#L34)
5. **Enforce scanner event, gate and window policy (TKT-03/05).** Bind admission to the server-confirmed assignment and time window on every supported entry path. Retest event and device permissions, revocation, repeat and concurrent online admission, connectivity loss, overrides and audit evidence with two scanner sessions. Resolve offline scope and retest the sync/conflict workflow if enabled. [Scanner routes](../routes/organizer.php#L22) · [Admission handler](../app/Http/Controllers/BackEnd/Organizer/OrganizerController.php#L596)
6. **Close operational release gates.** Verify minute-level scheduler coverage and fresh heartbeats from `payments` and `default`. Collect account recovery and MFA evidence, run the approved staging load and isolated restore drills, and verify on-call delivery and sanitized audit records. After the candidate passes staging, collect the production configuration preflight, Finance and Operations approvals, and separately authorized low-value live smoke and reconciliation evidence before go-live. [Launch evidence checklist](../docs/production-launch-evidence-checklist.md) · [Recovery runbook](../docs/backup-and-recovery-runbook.md)
